About ZSecura

Built in Sri Lanka. Trusted everywhere.

ZSecura is a professional cybersecurity consulting and compliance services firm headquartered in Colombo, Sri Lanka. We work with organisations across South Asia to reduce risk, achieve regulatory compliance, and build security programs that actually work — not just on audit day, but every day.

Our Story

Founded by security professionals who spent years inside enterprise security teams, ZSecura was built on a simple belief: cybersecurity should be practical, not theatrical. We have seen organisations spend millions on tools that sit unused, and we have seen teams pass audits while remaining fundamentally insecure. We do neither. Every recommendation we make is something we would implement ourselves — and have.
